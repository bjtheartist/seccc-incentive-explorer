# Program catalog audit — October 9, 2026

Release scope: `audit/program-catalog-2026-10-09`, based on the published CDG/NSF/DCEO release `56c7607`. Billy authorized push and publication of these audit corrections on October 9. Deployment evidence will be appended after live verification.

## Scope and result

All 89 catalog records received a scoped review of administrator sources, headline terms, intake information and directory placement. The initial link inventory covered 133 distinct source, contact and application URLs. Confirmed corrections affect 23 records. The [record ledger](program-catalog-2026-10-09.csv) identifies each change and the limits of each unchanged review. The [initial link ledger](program-links-2026-10-09.csv) preserves retrieval results before replacement URLs were applied.

This is not an all-clear certification of every ordinance, manual, eligibility condition or live funding balance. Five records have unresolved current intake evidence: Cook County Catalyst, Cook County Small Business Source, EDA Build to Scale, legacy Illinois Wet Lab Capital and legacy IIVF. Several other records deliberately already have unknown intake, particularly investment and lender-delivered resources. Unchanged records did not receive blanket new verification dates.

The initial link pass returned 122 accessible HTML responses, two accessible PDFs and nine blocked responses (403/406). It found no hard 404 or 5xx response. Some successful responses were empty JavaScript shells or redirects to a general directory; they were not counted as proof of current terms. Rendered ComEd pages and primary PDFs supplied evidence where plain HTML did not.

## Ordering and discovery

CDG Small, Medium and Large remain first in the directory. NSF remains included at the bottom. Report discovery still elevates Small CDG only when both mapped TIF/SBIF and NOF coverage are explicitly negative; missing coverage does not imply eligibility or trigger that rule. TIF remains the existing SBIF geography proxy.

The printable overview now respects CDG/NSF priority within categories. A Nonprofit / CDFI filter and overview section expose all three existing nonprofit lenders. The 15 current DCEO resources remain separately searchable under State; the three historical resources remain behind the inactive toggle. These categories do not imply that every resource is a cash grant.

The public projection now withdraws an old Open/Current claim once its published window passes. It uses “unknown—confirm” rather than inferring a permanent closure. Detail-page headers also distinguish unknown intake and unconfirmed openings from a confirmed closed round. Date-only deadlines include the entire Chicago closing day; exact times honor their offset. The change preserves the administrator-review date and does not turn today's render time into new verification evidence.
