# Information needed for a workable grant match

The business questionnaire records business name, business type, industry, primary goal and intake date, plus role, entity category, operating stage, location, expenses, budget, acceptable funding types, reimbursement readiness and an optional target funding date. The grant record needs comparable, source-backed information.

| Information                | What to capture                                                                                                                             | Matching use                                                                                    |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Program identity           | Program name, funder, public/philanthropic/corporate/community category, official program URL                                               | Stable record across funding rounds; deduplication                                              |
| Funding form               | Cash grant, reimbursement, tax benefit, loan or in-kind support                                                                             | Respect the business's preferences and upfront-cash constraints                                 |
| Timing                     | One-time/recurring/hybrid; each round's opening, deadline with timezone, and published status                                               | Distinguish current applications, future windows and closed rounds                              |
| Applicant eligibility      | Roles, legal entity category, business types, industries and operating stages                                                               | Compare with intake answers; preserve unknowns                                                  |
| Location                   | Eligible city/area/zone and the exact source describing it                                                                                  | Compare confirmed geography; address alone is not a zone verification                           |
| Eligible expenses          | Explicit categories, exclusions and whether mixed-use buildings or particular repairs are covered                                           | Compare project costs; flag partially covered scopes                                            |
| Amount and cash conditions | Award range/cap, percentage covered, minimum spend, matching funds, reimbursement rules and payment timing                                  | Staff checks against the budget and target date; currently captured as amount/requirements text |
| Other conditions           | Revenue/employee limits, years operating, licenses, taxes, permits, tenant/owner rules, documentation, stacking and work-start restrictions | Explicit manual review questions; not inferred from incomplete records                          |
| Evidence and maintenance   | Official round/application URL, supporting evidence, verification date, next review date, responsible teammate and notes                    | Keep matches current and explain changes                                                        |

**Minimum to research:** a program name, funder and official source. It can be saved as an unverified lead while details are missing.

**Minimum to surface a relevant lead:** a recorded round, useful cost criteria and enough known alignment to screen it. Unverified rounds remain in the research section. Blank eligibility fields mean unknown; `any` means the source explicitly supports unrestricted eligibility in that category.

**Minimum to recommend:** current verified availability, source-backed rules, resolved applicant questions, a documented staff rationale and next action. A grant deadline does not establish an award or payment date. Recurrence does not prove a new round is open. Internal approval does not submit an application or send a message.

Industry and business-type entries currently use recorded text. Exact alignment improves ranking; unmatched terms require review because synonyms are not proof of ineligibility. Role, entity, stage and funding-form fields use structured choices. Financial thresholds and narrative conditions remain manual checks until they have structured source-backed fields and matching logic.
