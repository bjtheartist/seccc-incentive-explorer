Internal draft for Billy review. Do not send yet.

## Pilot Goal

Recruit 12 people with real Chicago projects to complete an address report and tell us whether the report led to a useful next step. This is a product-learning cohort, not a financing or incentive application cohort.

## Cohort

- 4 business owners planning equipment, renovation, expansion, or relocation
- 3 property owners or developers considering rehabilitation, mixed-use work, or vacant-property acquisition
- 3 businesses referred by a financing or business-support organization
- 2 public or community reviewers who can assess whether the language respects separate public, community, program, and financing roles

Participants should have a real Chicago address and a project they are considering within the next 12 months. Do not ask for financial documents or sensitive business information during recruitment.

## Feedback Questions

1. Did the report give you a useful next step? If yes, what was it?
2. Did any organization listing feel confusing, too directive, or like an endorsement?
3. What did you expect to be able to do after reading the report?

## Pilot Success Signals

- 12 completed report sessions across at least four project types
- At least 8 participants answer the first two feedback questions
- No participant interprets an organization listing as approval, endorsement, or guaranteed eligibility
- At least 3 participants take a useful next action, such as saving the report, contacting a resource, or preparing a question
- Every reported incorrect or unclear organization listing is reviewed before expanding the cohort
