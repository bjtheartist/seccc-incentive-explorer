# Project Vetting Partner Pilot

Internal draft for Billy review. Do not circulate yet.

## Purpose

Chicago Incentive Explorer helps a business or property owner understand which public programs reach a project address. The pilot adds the professional next step: when a report involves financing, acquisition, construction, remodeling, expansion, or equipment, it introduces one mission-aligned project vetting partner whose service area and capabilities fit the request.

The Explorer does not prequalify a borrower, promise funding, estimate an award, or replace underwriting. The partner makes every lending and project-viability determination.

## User Experience

1. The user enters a project address and describes the work they are considering.
2. The report identifies relevant incentive programs without presenting a top-line dollar estimate.
3. For a capital-shaped project, the report names one project vetting partner and explains why the organization may be a useful starting point.
4. The user can visit the partner's public intake path or explicitly request an introduction.
5. No business profile, report, document, or contact information is shared with a partner without the user's consent.

## Partner Role

Pilot partners are asked to:

- Confirm their service geography, project types, borrower types, and preferred intake path.
- Review the language used to describe their role.
- Identify information that would make an initial project conversation more productive.
- Flag stale or incorrect information through a correction path.
- Join one short feedback conversation after the pilot begins.

Partners are not asked to guarantee response times, approvals, financing, or eligibility.

## What We Measure

- Partner handoff shown
- Public intake link clicked
- Introduction requested with consent
- Partner acknowledgment, when reported

These are engagement and referral-intent measures. They are not represented as applications, approvals, funded projects, or dollars deployed.

## Editorial Guardrails

- Service geography and project fit come before physical proximity.
- Internal matching logic and scores are never shown to users.
- No paid placement or payment for rank.
- No funding amount or approval promise.
- Partner information includes a source and verification date.
- Users can suggest a correction or missing organization.
- The user chooses whether any information is shared.

## Proposed Four-Week Pilot

- Week 1: Confirm partner profiles, intake links, and client-facing language.
- Week 2: Test report matching and handoff tracking on a private preview.
- Week 3: Launch with a small group of partner-referred businesses and chamber users.
- Week 4: Review engagement, incorrect matches, user questions, and partner feedback.

## Partner Conversation

The most useful first meeting is 30 minutes:

- 10 minutes: live report and handoff walkthrough
- 10 minutes: partner service and product boundaries
- 5 minutes: preferred intake and consent process
- 5 minutes: pilot decision and next owner
