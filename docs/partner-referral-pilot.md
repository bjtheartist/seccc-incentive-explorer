# Local Support Introduction Pilot

Internal draft for Billy review. Do not circulate yet.

## Purpose

Chicago Incentive Explorer helps a business or property owner understand which public programs reach a project address. The pilot adds a path to local support: when a report involves financing, acquisition, construction, remodeling, expansion, or equipment, it surfaces organizations whose publicly listed service area and capabilities may be relevant to the request and helps the owner prepare an introduction.

The Explorer does not endorse an organization, prequalify a borrower, promise funding, estimate an award, or replace underwriting or any public, community, agency, or program review. Each organization decides whether and how it can assist.

## User Experience

1. The user enters a project address and describes the work they are considering.
2. The report identifies relevant incentive programs without presenting a top-line dollar estimate.
3. For a capital-shaped project, the report surfaces one local support organization to explore and provides access to other resources.
4. The guide asks where the project stands and gathers enough context for a useful first conversation without assigning a readiness score or determining viability.
5. The user can visit an organization's public information or intake path or explicitly request an introduction.
6. The user reviews the project summary and chooses exactly what may be shared.
7. No business profile, report, document, or contact information is shared with an organization without the user's consent.

## Participating Organization Role

Pilot organizations are asked to:

- Confirm their service geography, project types, borrower types, and preferred intake path.
- Review the language used to describe their role.
- Identify information that would make an initial project conversation more productive.
- Flag stale or incorrect information through a correction path.
- Join one short feedback conversation after the pilot begins.

Participating organizations are not asked to guarantee response times, approvals, financing, or eligibility.

## What We Measure

- Resource listing shown
- Public intake link clicked
- Introduction requested with consent
- Organization acknowledgment, when reported

These are engagement and referral-intent measures. They are not represented as applications, approvals, funded projects, or dollars deployed.

## Editorial Guardrails

- Service geography and project fit come before physical proximity.
- Internal matching logic and scores are never shown to users.
- No paid placement or payment for rank.
- No funding amount or approval promise.
- Organization information includes a source and verification date.
- Financing organizations drawn from the U.S. Treasury CDFI Fund certified list carry that certification verbatim as a factual, sourced label. Certification is federal provenance, not an Explorer endorsement, deal role, or approval; copy never calls any organization an "official" financier of a project.
- Membership-based consumer credit unions on the certified list are not routed to business or development reports; they remain in the outreach directory until a consumer-facing surface exists.
- Users can suggest a correction or missing organization.
- The user chooses whether any information is shared.
- Missing nonessential details remain open questions and do not block an initial conversation.
- Public copy says "Get connected to local support" and never labels the user or project as ready, viable, approved, financeable, or eligible.

## Proposed Four-Week Pilot

- Week 1: Confirm organization profiles, intake links, and client-facing language.
- Week 2: Test report matching and handoff tracking on a private preview.
- Week 3: Launch with a small group of organization-referred businesses and chamber users.
- Week 4: Review engagement, incorrect matches, user questions, and organization feedback.

## Organization Conversation

The most useful first meeting is 30 minutes:

- 10 minutes: live report and handoff walkthrough
- 10 minutes: organization service and product boundaries
- 5 minutes: preferred intake and consent process
- 5 minutes: pilot decision and next owner
