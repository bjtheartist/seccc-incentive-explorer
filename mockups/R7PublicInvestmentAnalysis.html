What this suggests

Illustrative awarded-grant records total $142M from 2020–2026. Philanthropic foundations account for 30.1% of awarded grants, and the three leading aggregated recipient names account for 65.6% of philanthropic awarded dollars.

What this cannot prove

This analysis cannot establish whether funds were received, are currently available, remain unspent, or produced completed projects or outcomes. It does not establish neighborhood-wide benefit, causal impact, or eligibility for any program.

Methodology

How to read the illustrative brief

  • The six capital classes describe different instruments and are never combined.
  • The awarded-grant trend uses the same annual window for philanthropic and other awarded-grant dollars.
  • The illustrative philanthropic yearly values sum to $42.7M and reconcile with the philanthropic funder row.
  • Recipient names are aggregated before ranking. The illustrative top-three share is $28.0M ÷ $42.7M = 65.6%.
  • Unavailable values stay unavailable; the prototype does not substitute $0.
