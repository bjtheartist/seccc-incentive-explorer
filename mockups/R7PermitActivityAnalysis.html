What this suggests

Illustrative filing activity rises from 132 filings in 2020 to a peak of 246 in 2024, then remains similar in 2025. Repair and alteration filings lead the type mix, and most source-recorded statuses are “Issued.”

What this cannot prove

These records do not establish construction start, completion, current activity, occupancy, code compliance, or project value. Filings may be amended, withdrawn, or superseded, and no record does not imply that no work occurred.
