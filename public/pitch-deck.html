Who This Hurts
01
The business owner who renovates out of pocket — not knowing SBIF could have reimbursed up to $150,000 of that work.
02
The entrepreneur who hears “you might qualify for something” — but finds no clear path from that sentence to an answer. So they give up.
03
The result: incentives designed for underinvested communities go unclaimed — or flow to businesses that already have consultants and lawyers.
What It Is

Chicago Incentive Explorer is a public tool that helps businesses identify location-based incentives and take the first next step.

One address. Clear results. Actionable next steps.

CSIM doesn't replace agencies.
It replaces the scavenger hunt.

How It Works

From address to action
in four steps

1
Enter your address
Cross-referenced against 11 incentive zone layers using geocoded boundary data from federal, state, and city sources.
2
Answer quick questions
Industry, business type, and intent narrow results so you can focus on the most relevant programs.
3
Get your report
Downloadable PDF with program names, benefits, who administers each, how to apply, and direct contacts.
4
Take the first step
Direct links to apply, agency contact information, and SECCC support if you want help navigating what comes next.
The Landscape

What exists today —
and where it falls short

Government websites
Authoritative, but disconnected across agencies
Single-layer maps
Show zones, not incentives or next steps
Open data portals
Powerful, but not accessible to business owners
Property platforms
Property-first, not incentive-first
Commercial RE platforms
Professional-grade, paywalled
High-touch advisors
Accurate, but expensive and uneven access
Where CSIM Fits

The missing middle layer.

A public, Chicago-specific incentive navigation tool that turns address + basic business context into clear results and a first next step.

Backed by SECCC — a trusted community institution, not a faceless platform
Maintained with program partners — agency contacts verify and update listings
Free and open — no subscription, no paywall, accessible to every Chicago business
